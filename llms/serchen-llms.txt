## What Serchen is

Serchen is a demand-side discovery surface, not a software vendor with a product API.
Buyers browse categories and industry hubs, compare products side by side, and read
user reviews. Vendors claim a listing and optionally pay for placement. Everything a
machine could want from Serchen is served as HTML pages behind a Cloudflare edge.

## Machine-access policy

Serchen serves Content-Signal directives in
[robots.txt](https://www.serchen.com/robots.txt): `search=yes, ai-train=no,
use=reference`. It additionally disallows Amazonbot, Applebot-Extended, Bytespider,
CCBot, ClaudeBot, CloudflareBrowserRenderingCrawler, Google-Extended, GPTBot and
meta-externalagent. Note the tension: Serchen markets its listings as boosting "AI
discovery inside Google, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini" while blocking the crawlers
those systems use. Ordinary browser requests to serchen.com pass; unbranded automated
clients are frequently served a Cloudflare interstitial (HTTP 403).
